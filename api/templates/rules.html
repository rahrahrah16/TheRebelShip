Flight manual · Pilot guide

How to play

Collect asteroid resources, build helper ships and grow your fleet before the rival commander arrives.

Controls

  • W, A, S, D or arrow keys — move
  • J or left mouse button — shoot
  • Space — open or close the Control Center
  • P — pause; F11 — fullscreen; Esc — quit

How to win

Shoot asteroids to collect Iron, Copper, Crystal and Power. Assign helper ships to mine or attack. Buy upgrades in the Tech Lab, then defeat the commander. Your ship is lost if its hull reaches zero.
